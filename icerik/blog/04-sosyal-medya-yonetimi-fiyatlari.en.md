**Social media management is priced as a monthly service because planning, production, approval, publishing and reporting repeat every month.** A useful quotation must state the number of platforms and deliverables, not only a monthly amount.

GZL Teknoloji currently offers TRY-denominated packages for the Turkish market. The key purpose of the examples below is to make scope differences transparent.

## What does management include?

A complete workflow may contain:

1. channel and competitor review;
2. monthly content planning;
3. copywriting and visual preparation;
4. approval and revision;
5. publishing or scheduling;
6. community-response guidance;
7. performance reporting;
8. advertising preparation or campaign support.

Not every package includes every step. Ask whether “12 posts” means twelve finished designs and captions, whether video editing is included, and who responds to comments and messages.

## Agency, freelancer or in-house employee?

| Model | Strength | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer | Direct communication and flexible scope | Capacity may depend on one person |
| Agency or specialist team | Multiple disciplines and repeatable process | Requires a clear approval workflow |
| In-house | Deep daily brand knowledge | Salary, tools and management overhead |

The right model depends on how much work must happen every week. A small business with a limited publishing plan may not need a full-time employee. A high-volume brand may need an internal owner even when production is outsourced.

## Why the cheapest package can become expensive

A low headline price often excludes the tasks that consume the most time: revisions, short-form video, reporting, community management or paid-campaign assets. Those tasks are later billed separately or remain undone.

Before choosing, request answers to these questions:

- How many channels and deliverables are included?
- Are captions, design and video editing included?
- How many revision rounds are available?
- Is publishing included or only file delivery?
- Who owns the source files and account access?
- What does the monthly report measure?
- Are advertising budget and advertising management separate?

## Management service versus automation software

These are related but different products. A management service provides strategy and creative work. Automation software centralizes scheduling, approvals, account access and reporting.

A custom automation dashboard can reduce operational time for businesses managing several brands or platforms. It does not create a strategy by itself; it makes an established process faster and more measurable.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is advertising spend included in the management fee?

Usually not. Media spend is paid to the advertising platform. Creative preparation and campaign management must be listed separately in the proposal.

### How quickly can results be expected?

Publishing consistency can improve immediately, while commercial outcomes depend on the offer, audience, budget and sales process. A responsible provider should define measurable indicators instead of guaranteeing follower or revenue numbers.

### Who owns the accounts and content?

The customer should retain ownership of business accounts and grant role-based access. Ownership of editable design files should be clarified before work begins.
