# Cookie Policy

**Effective date: 11 July 2026**

This policy explains cookies and similar storage technologies used on gzlteknoloji.com. A cookie is a small data record stored by your browser.

## 1. Cookie categories

**Necessary cookies** support secure operation and remember preferences. They cannot be disabled through the website. **Analytics cookies** help us understand website use and are activated only with consent.

## 2. Cookies used

| Cookie | Category | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `site_cookie_consent_v1` | Necessary | GZL Technology | Remember cookie and analytics preferences | 180 days |
| `_ga` | Analytics | Google Analytics | Distinguish users | Up to 2 years |
| `_ga_<container-id>` | Analytics | Google Analytics | Persist session state | Up to 2 years |

The container identifier in the analytics cookie name depends on the active Google Analytics measurement identifier. Browsers may enforce shorter lifetimes.

## 3. Managing consent

Analytics cookies are disabled by default. Your choice is stored in the necessary preference cookie and browser local storage for 180 days. Refusing analytics does not prevent essential website functions.

Cookies may also be deleted or blocked in browser settings. Deleting the necessary preference cookie may cause the consent interface to appear again. Analytics preferences may be changed through cookie settings.

## 4. Analytics and transfers

Where analytics consent is given, Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager may be used. Device and usage data may be processed by Google outside Türkiye. See the Privacy Policy for further information.

## 5. Updates and contact

This policy is updated when tools or applicable law changes. Questions may be sent to info@gzlteknoloji.com.
